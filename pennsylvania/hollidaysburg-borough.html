Paving FAQ — Hollidaysburg, Pennsylvania

How often should I sealcoat my driveway in Hollidaysburg?
Sealcoating a Hollidaysburg asphalt driveway every 2-3 years is the standard recommendation. New asphalt should be sealed after 6-12 months (once fully cured). Sealcoating fills surface oxidation, blocks UV damage and prevents water infiltration — the main cause of freeze-thaw cracking in Pennsylvania's climate. At $219-$796 per application, regular sealcoating is the most cost-effective maintenance you can do to protect your $2390+ driveway investment.
When is the best time to pave a driveway in Hollidaysburg, PA?
Late spring (May-June) through early fall (September-October) is ideal for asphalt paving in Hollidaysburg — temperatures above 50°F are required for proper hot-mix asphalt compaction and curing. Avoid paving in winter or when rain is forecast. Many Hollidaysburg paving contractors book up in summer, so scheduling your free estimate in early spring ensures preferred timing. Sealcoating should be done when daytime temps are consistently above 55°F.
What thickness should an asphalt driveway be in Pennsylvania?
For a residential driveway in Hollidaysburg, the standard is 2-3 inches of compacted hot-mix asphalt over a 4-6 inch compacted gravel base. The base is as important as the asphalt — a properly compacted gravel base prevents settling, cracking and drainage issues in Pennsylvania's frost-prone climate. Driveways used by heavy vehicles (trucks, RVs) should have a thicker asphalt layer (3-4 inches). Our Hollidaysburg contractors provide material specs with every estimate.
How much does a new driveway cost in Hollidaysburg, PA?
A new asphalt driveway in Hollidaysburg typically costs between $2390 and $6970 for an average-sized residential driveway (600-1,500 sq ft), including excavation, gravel base, hot-mix asphalt and grading. Larger driveways or those requiring significant excavation or drainage work cost more. Sealcoating typically adds $219-$796. Get a free written estimate from a licensed Hollidaysburg paving contractor before committing to any work.
How long does an asphalt driveway last in Pennsylvania?
A properly installed asphalt driveway in Hollidaysburg's climate typically lasts 20-30 years with proper maintenance. Pennsylvania's freeze-thaw cycles are hard on asphalt — proper base prep and drainage are critical to longevity. Sealcoating every 2-3 years adds 5-10 years to the surface life. Crack filling promptly when cracks appear prevents water from penetrating the base. Our Hollidaysburg contractors use proper base depth (4-6 inches of compacted gravel) for maximum lifespan.
How long does a new driveway installation take in Hollidaysburg?
Most residential asphalt driveways in Hollidaysburg, PA are paved in 1-2 days. However, new installations requiring full excavation and gravel base installation may take 2-3 days total. The asphalt itself needs 24-48 hours to cure before driving on it, and full cure (for heavy vehicles) takes up to 2 weeks in Pennsylvania's summer heat. Your contractor will walk you through the specific timeline before work begins.
