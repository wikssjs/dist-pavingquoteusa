Paving FAQ — West Earl, Pennsylvania

How long does a new driveway installation take in West Earl?
Most residential asphalt driveways in West Earl, PA are paved in 1-2 days. However, new installations requiring full excavation and gravel base installation may take 2-3 days total. The asphalt itself needs 24-48 hours to cure before driving on it, and full cure (for heavy vehicles) takes up to 2 weeks in Pennsylvania's summer heat. Your contractor will walk you through the specific timeline before work begins.
When is the best time to pave a driveway in West Earl, PA?
Late spring (May-June) through early fall (September-October) is ideal for asphalt paving in West Earl — temperatures above 50°F are required for proper hot-mix asphalt compaction and curing. Avoid paving in winter or when rain is forecast. Many West Earl paving contractors book up in summer, so scheduling your free estimate in early spring ensures preferred timing. Sealcoating should be done when daytime temps are consistently above 55°F.
How much does a new driveway cost in West Earl, PA?
A new asphalt driveway in West Earl typically costs between $2447 and $7138 for an average-sized residential driveway (600-1,500 sq ft), including excavation, gravel base, hot-mix asphalt and grading. Larger driveways or those requiring significant excavation or drainage work cost more. Sealcoating typically adds $224-$815. Get a free written estimate from a licensed West Earl paving contractor before committing to any work.
What thickness should an asphalt driveway be in Pennsylvania?
For a residential driveway in West Earl, the standard is 2-3 inches of compacted hot-mix asphalt over a 4-6 inch compacted gravel base. The base is as important as the asphalt — a properly compacted gravel base prevents settling, cracking and drainage issues in Pennsylvania's frost-prone climate. Driveways used by heavy vehicles (trucks, RVs) should have a thicker asphalt layer (3-4 inches). Our West Earl contractors provide material specs with every estimate.
How do I find a licensed paving contractor in West Earl, PA?
In Pennsylvania, paving contractors should carry a valid PA Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration for residential work, plus general liability and workers' compensation insurance. When hiring in West Earl: verify their HIC registration number; ask for a certificate of insurance naming you; get at least two written estimates with material specs; check Google reviews and the BBB. Our network includes only pre-screened, licensed and insured West Earl paving contractors.
Should I repair or replace my driveway in West Earl?
